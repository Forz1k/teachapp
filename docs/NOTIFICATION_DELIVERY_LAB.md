# Delivery scheduling LAB

Pure internal claim/finish helpers over the existing synthetic member notice; not connected to public routes, timers, Durable Object alarms or a real sender. The caller must persist a claim atomically before any transport. One 30-second (or shorter expiry-bound) lease per notice sequence, exact subscription revision/sequence/attempt/opaque ID fence. A crashed or expired claim becomes unknown, not automatically replayed; explicit bounded retry hints allow at most three attempts within expiry. Accepted transport does not acknowledge the inbox. A gone subscription is disabled only if its claim is still current, and a replacement consent/newer sequence cannot be overwritten. JSON round-trip, stale claim, crash, expiry, retries and revocation are tested. The helpers are a policy rehearsal, not a durable scheduler implementation or proof of delivery. No endpoint/key material exposed by the ticket.

## Local Worker transactional rehearsal

Owner-only POST notification-lab/delivery/claim, /check and /finish exercise the internal policy inside the existing workspace SQL transaction. Claim requires exact member, subscription revision and notice sequence; server-generated opaque ticket from a fresh issued hash, no endpoint/key in responses. Check and finish require the exact ticket. Finish is an operator-supplied simulated outcome, not verified delivery; no network, scheduler or acknowledgement. Separate workerd smoke races two claims, restarts with the same storage, checks the persisted ticket and finishes it once. Unit tests exercise lease expiry and stale sequences, forbidden actors, workspace isolation and no acknowledgement. All routes are LAB only, rate-limited as access changes, never available in production configuration.
